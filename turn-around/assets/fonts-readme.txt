Fonts on this page

noto-sans-regular.woff and noto-sans-display-bold.woff are Noto Sans Regular and Noto Sans Display Bold,
cut down to Latin text (other glyph outlines and the GSUB and DSIG tables removed; the remaining outlines
are unchanged) and wrapped as WOFF. Noto is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; see
Noto-OFL-1.1.txt in this folder.

turn-block.ttf is not a Noto font. It is the block alphabet drawn for the Turn Around music video
(the polygons in the film's letters.js), built into a font for this page, with a question mark,
a few other marks and numerals added in the same manner.
